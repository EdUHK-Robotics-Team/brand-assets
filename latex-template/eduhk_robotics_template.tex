\eyebrow{Overview}
\section{Template structure}

This template translates the EdUHK Robotics Team's web/CSS visual language into a practical A4 format for technical documentation. It combines the team's green accent system, official horizontal logo, clean sans-serif typography, reusable cards, tables, and a light-blue code area.

\begin{infobox}[Included code example]
The example in this document is the \textbf{Robocon 2026 EdUHK Robotics Team R2 Arduino IMU and encoder receiver program}. It is reproduced from \texttt{enc\_imu\_may31.ino} and credited to \textbf{Steven Zhang Yancheng}.\\
\textbf{Source:} \href{\CodeSourceURL}{Steven-ZYC/Robocon2026\_r2 on GitHub (dev branch)}
\end{infobox}

\subsection{Program role}

The Arduino sketch reads two quadrature encoders, parses LPBUS IMU packets received on \texttt{Serial3}, combines heading, angular rate, acceleration, and encoder counts into one payload, and appends a CRC-8/ATM checksum before serial output.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}l X@{}}
\toprule
Area & Implementation \\
\midrule
Controller & Arduino Mega 2560 serial and interrupt interfaces \\
Encoders & Two AMT103 quadrature channels, decoded at four times resolution \\
IMU & LPBUS packet parser on \texttt{Serial3} at 115200 baud \\
Output & Timestamped IMU/encoder payload with CRC-8/ATM \\
Credit & Steven Zhang Yancheng, Robocon 2026 EdUHK Robotics Team R2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
