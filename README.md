# EdUHK Robotics Team Brand Assets

This repository stores commonly used brand assets of the EdUHK Robotics Team, particularly logos and graphics used in our publicity and communications materials.

Its primary purpose is to support our existing publicity workflow by keeping frequently used assets in one place, making them easier for team members to access and reuse across different materials and platforms.

## Use of Assets

Assets in this repository are primarily maintained for the EdUHK Robotics Team's internal publicity and communications work.

The availability of an asset in this repository does **not** constitute permission or authorization for its use by external parties. Prior authorization from the relevant organization or department **must be obtained before any asset is used, reproduced, modified, or redistributed**.

### EdUHK Robotics Team

Members of the general public who wish to use the EdUHK Robotics Team logo or other related assets must obtain prior authorization from the team.

Please contact:

**EdUHK Robotics Team**  
[robotics@eduhk.hk](mailto:robotics@eduhk.hk)

### Departmental Assets

Assets belonging to the **Department of Mathematics and Information Technology (MIT)** or the **Department of Science and Environmental Studies (SES)** remain under the respective department.

Authorization must be obtained directly from the relevant department before use.

**Department of Mathematics and Information Technology (MIT)**  
[mit@eduhk.hk](mailto:mit@eduhk.hk)

**Department of Science and Environmental Studies (SES)**  
[dses@eduhk.hk](mailto:dses@eduhk.hk)

### University-level Assets

For the use of university-level logos, branding, or other official assets of **The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK)**, authorization must be sought from the University through the **Communications Office**.

**Communications Office**  
[co@eduhk.hk](mailto:co@eduhk.hk)

Please do not use any of the above assets unless and until the appropriate authorization has been granted.
